    What the numbers mean

    small sample

    All PnL is paper: 1.0 level-stake units per bet. Negative pilot results are reported as-is - a 27-match sample cannot separate skill from luck, and nothing here is betting advice.

    Competition board

    Paper leaderboard

    Transparent rankings by verified level-stakes profit. Review-state PnL is shown but demoted: unaudited numbers never outrank verified ones.

    i
    Small-sample warning.

    Each entrant has far fewer than 50 settled bets. Profit, ROI and strike rate at this size are dominated by variance - the bootstrap confidence intervals on the Strategy lab show how wide the honest error bars are.

    !
    OLBG imports are pending by design.

    OLBG's Terms of Use permit manual personal review only. Imported consensus tips carry no permissioned odds and no permissioned final-result path, so they can never enter verified PnL. They are shown so the source behaviour can be studied, never to look settled.

    Verification states

    What each label means

    verifiedidentity confirmed by an independent second source agreeing on the final result (27/27 on the football pilot)
    probablesingle permissioned source only — graded on accuracy, never ranked as verified PnL
    reviewa settlement or desk carrying an unresolved irregularity; shown, demoted, never hidden
    blockedno permissioned results path exists for this sport in this repository

    Protocol

    Settlement gates

    Seven gates run before any settlement: identity, pre-start time, market rule set, final result existence, single consistent scoreline, provider permission, and rule version. Postponed → pending, cancelled/abandoned → void, disputed → withheld with anomalies. There is no force-settle path.

    All 21 OLBG sport families

    Sport coverage & source gates

    Every sport family OLBG publishes, with the candidate official result source, the exact robots.txt text that was fetched (and when), the licence state, and the pre-registered strategy designs waiting behind the gate. A gate is computed from those facts in code - it cannot be promoted by editing prose. Nothing here is a coverage claim: the only sports with a live results path today are the three the OpenLigaDB ODbL licence permits.

    i
    How to read this page.

    “robots.txt allows/partial/blocks” is the site's own crawling policy, fetched verbatim - it is not a licence. “Licence: not reviewed” means exactly that: no permission, no collection. Only permitted_open_licence (a licence already recorded and tested in the code's policy registry) lets a collector run.
