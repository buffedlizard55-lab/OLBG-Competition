Built for review

From tip to trusted result

no shortcuts / no invented outcomes
01

Collect

Capture a tip with its original URL, event ID, publication time, market, selection, odds, and tipster identity.

02

Verify

Match the event to an official competition result. Conflicts, voids, missing odds, and late edits go to review.

03

Settle

Calculate level-stakes PnL only after the result is verified. Keep a permanent provenance trail for every number.
